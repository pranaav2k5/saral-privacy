## What personal data we process and why

We ask for your consent to process the personal data listed below, for each purpose.

1. Product updates

   Personal data: Email address, Name
   How long we keep it: Until you withdraw consent, then 30 days

2. Analytics
   What we do: Understand how the app is used so we can improve it.
   Personal data: Device type, Pages visited

## Who we share it with

We share your personal data with: Example Analytics Ltd.

## Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to:
- withdraw your consent at any time;
- get a summary of the personal data we process and of the other parties we have shared it with;
- ask us to correct, complete or update your personal data, or to erase it;
- have your grievance handled by us;
- nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or cannot act yourself.
To use these rights, go to https://example.org/privacy/requests or write to privacy@example.org.

## How to withdraw your consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, at https://example.org/privacy/withdraw. Withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew.

## Children's personal data

We do not knowingly process personal data of children (people under 18).

## How to complain

Contact us first: privacy@example.org, or write to: 1 Example Road, Bengaluru 560001, India. We will respond to your grievance. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
